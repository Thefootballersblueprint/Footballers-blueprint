What is The Footballers Blueprint?

The Footballers Blueprint is a Skool community built for young footballers who are serious about developing every part of their game. It brings together everything learned from professionals within football — nutrition, mindset, video analysis, positioning, drills, fitness, strength, and recovery — in one structured place, so players and parents aren't left piecing advice together from scattered videos and guesswork.

Whatever stage a player is at, the goal is the same: give them a clear, professional-level path to develop on and off the pitch.

What happens when you join

Your journey from day one, and beyond.

1

Welcome & Introduction

The team welcomes you personally. You introduce yourself — who you are, what you feel you need to work on, and where you're currently at in your footballing journey.

2

See the Next Level

Based on what you share, our professionals walk you through what the next level looks like for your game — the standards, habits, and skills that separate good players from those on a professional pathway.

3

Build Your Plan

Working with the community, we put together a personalised development plan — covering exactly what you need to focus on, whether that's technical skills, mindset, fitness, or recovery.

4

Keep Developing

Ongoing access to the full community, content, and support as you work through your plan and progress.
